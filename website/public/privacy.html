Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

At Half Price Hub, we are committed to transparency about the data we collect and how we use it. We collect information in the following ways:

Information You Provide

When you submit a half price order request, we collect:

  • Personal Information: Full name, email address, and delivery address
  • Order Details: Product links, size preferences, and payment method preferences
  • Additional Information: Social media links and contact information (optional)

Automatically Collected Information

We automatically collect certain information when you visit our website:

  • IP Address: Your internet protocol address for location identification
  • Geographic Information: Country, region, city, and timezone based on your IP
  • Device Information: Browser type, operating system, and screen resolution
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent on site, and interaction patterns
  • ISP Information: Your internet service provider details

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following legitimate business purposes:

Order Processing

  • To process and fulfill your half price product requests
  • To communicate with you about your order status
  • To arrange discounted purchases and delivery

Website Analytics

  • To understand visitor demographics and website usage patterns
  • To improve our website functionality and user experience
  • To maintain basic statistics for operational purposes

Communication

  • To respond to your inquiries and provide customer support
  • To send important updates about your orders
  • To contact you regarding your half price requests

3. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We take your privacy seriously and do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Information is only shared in the following circumstances:

Order Fulfillment

When you submit an order request, we may share relevant order information with product vendors or suppliers solely for the purpose of arranging your discounted purchase.

Legal Requirements

We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or government regulation, or to protect our rights and safety.

Service Providers

We may use third-party services for website hosting and analytics, but these providers are contractually obligated to maintain confidentiality.

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information:

  • Secure Storage: All data is stored using industry-standard encryption
  • Access Controls: Limited access to personal information on a need-to-know basis
  • Regular Updates: Security measures are regularly reviewed and updated
  • Incident Response: We have procedures in place to respond to potential security incidents

While we strive to protect your information, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide our services and fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy:

  • Order Information: Retained for 2 years after order completion for customer service and legal purposes
  • Analytics Data: Aggregated and anonymized data may be retained indefinitely
  • IP Addresses: Stored temporarily for security and analytics purposes

You may request deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting us.

6. Your Rights and Choices

You have certain rights regarding your personal information:

Access and Correction

You have the right to access, update, or correct your personal information.

Deletion

You can request deletion of your personal information, subject to legal and legitimate business requirements.

Opt-out

You can opt out of non-essential communications and analytics tracking.

Data Portability

You can request a copy of your personal data in a structured, machine-readable format.

7. Cookies and Tracking

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies for functionality and analytics:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for website operation and order processing
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how visitors use our site
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your settings and preferences

You can control cookie settings through your browser preferences.

8. Third-Party Services

Our website integrates with third-party services:

OpenStreetMap

We use OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service for address autocomplete. This service processes address queries but does not store personal information.

IP Geolocation

We use ipapi.co for IP-based geolocation services to determine visitor location and display country flags.

Discord Webhooks

Order notifications are sent to our private Discord server for internal processing and customer service.

9. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for international data transfers.

10. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected such information, we will take steps to delete it.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
